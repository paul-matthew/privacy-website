Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

Privacy Bridge is a privacy consulting practice operated by PM Daybreak Designs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you visit our website or contact us regarding our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, organization details, and any information included in messages submitted through our website or other communications.

We may also collect limited technical information related to website usage, such as browser type, device information, or website activity, where applicable.

How We Use Information

Personal information is used to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, schedule consultations, communicate with clients, and maintain business records.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Protection of Information

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Retention of Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or to meet applicable legal and business requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
