About Privacy Bridge

Privacy risk assessments and compliance support for private healthcare clinics

Who We Are

Privacy Bridge is a privacy consulting practice focused on helping private healthcare clinics understand and manage their obligations under PHIPA.

We combine hands-on privacy expertise with structured delivery to help clinics identify risk, document their data practices, and implement practical compliance processes.

What We Do

Most small clinics operate without formal privacy documentation or structured compliance processes. We help bridge that gap in a practical, non-technical way.

Our work focuses on how your clinic actually operates — reviewing systems, workflows, and data handling practices — and translating requirements into clear, actionable steps.

Why It Matters

Healthcare providers handle sensitive personal health information every day. Under PHIPA, clinics are responsible for ensuring appropriate safeguards are in place, including policies, staff training, breach response procedures, and vendor oversight.

Even small gaps can create unnecessary exposure. Our goal is to help clinics identify those gaps early and address them before they become compliance or reputational issues.
